Your account details, reading preferences and appearance are managed in separate settings pages.

## Name and email

Open **Settings → Profile**, edit **Name** or **Email address**, then select **Save**.

Changing your account email requires verification again. Scheduled digests pause until you verify the new address. The Profile page offers a link to resend the verification email.

Your email delivery destination is a separate setting under **Delivery**. Check it there if you want digests sent to a different address.

## Sign in with Google or GitHub

If your Pulse instance enables them, choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub** on the login or registration page.

The first sign-in links to your existing Pulse account when the provider supplies the same verified email address, regardless of letter case. That address is also verified in Pulse. Later sign-ins use the saved provider link. Google must report a verified email; GitHub must supply a public or primary verified email. If no account matches, Pulse creates one when registration is open. No verification email is needed for that account.

Two-factor authentication still applies when you sign in through Google or GitHub. Accounts created this way start without a password. If an existing account has not verified its email, linking by verified provider email clears its password, two-factor setup, passkeys and other connected accounts, and signs out its previous sessions. Verified accounts keep their password.

## Connect or disconnect an account

Open **Settings → Security → Connected accounts**. Each enabled provider offers **Connect** or **Disconnect**. Select **Connect** and approve access with the provider to link it to the Pulse account you are currently using, even when its email differs. A provider account already connected to another reader cannot be linked. Connecting requires a verified email, and if your account has a password, you may be asked to confirm it first.

Select **Disconnect** to remove a provider link. You must keep a password or another enabled connected provider so you can still sign in. Accounts with a password may be asked to confirm it first.

## Set or change your password

Open **Settings → Security**. If you signed up with Google or GitHub, the **Set password** form lets you choose a password without entering a current one. You can then use either email and password or a connected provider to sign in.

If you already have a password, use **Update password** and enter your current password. You can also choose **Forgot password?** on the login page and request a reset link for your Pulse email address.

## About you

Open **Settings → Digest → About you** to edit the text used to tailor AI content. It is optional, limited to 2,000 characters and sent to your AI provider. See [Write a good profile](/docs/your-digest/write-a-good-profile).

## Light and dark appearance

Open **Settings → Appearance** and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. System follows this device's appearance. The choice applies in this browser.
