Open **Settings → Security** to manage two-factor authentication and passkeys. If your account has a password, you may be asked to confirm it before entering this page. Accounts without a password can open Security and manage two-factor authentication and passkeys directly.

![Security settings, with two-factor authentication](/images/shots/security-desktop.webp)

## Enable two-factor authentication

1. Select **Enable 2FA**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the setup key manually.
3. Continue to verification, enter the app's six-digit code and select **Confirm**.

After setup, use **View recovery codes** and store the codes somewhere safe. Each code can be used once if you lose access to your authenticator. Generating new codes invalidates the previous ones.

You can return to Security and select **Disable 2FA** to turn it off.

## Add a passkey

Under **Passkeys**, select **Add passkey**, enter a **Passkey name**, then choose **Register passkey**. Follow your browser or device prompt to finish.

A passkey lets you sign in without entering your password. The page tells you if your browser does not support passkeys. You can remove a saved passkey from the list.

## Keep your password

Adding a passkey does not remove an existing password. The **Update password** form on Security lets you change it using your current password. If your account has no password, use **Set password** to add one without entering a current password. Connected Google and GitHub accounts remain available for sign-in.
