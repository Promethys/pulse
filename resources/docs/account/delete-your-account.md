You can permanently delete your account from **Settings → Profile**.

## Delete the account

1. Find **Delete account** on the Profile page.
2. Select **Delete account** to open the confirmation dialog.
3. Enter your current password.
4. Select **Delete account** again to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep the account.

Deletion signs you out and removes your account and its related records, including digest settings, personal keys, subscriptions, custom sources, saved posts and learning history.

> [!WARNING]
> Account deletion cannot be undone. Pulse has no data export feature.

## Copies outside Pulse

Deletion does not recall emails or remove messages already sent to Discord. Manage those copies in the destination service. To revoke an API key itself, use the provider's key management page.
