These answers cover common questions about setting up and reading your digest.

## Do you need an AI key?

Usually, yes. You can skip the key during onboarding, but scheduled digests need a personal key unless your account has instance AI access. See [Why you bring your own key](/docs/ai-keys/why-you-bring-your-own-key).

## Is the AI usage free?

Some providers have free tiers. Your provider may charge for generation and key tests, depending on your account and model. Check its own pricing and billing settings. See [What it costs](/docs/ai-keys/what-it-costs).

## Why has the digest not arrived at the chosen time?

Pulse checks every 15 minutes and then prepares due posts. Delivery can take longer. Check your timezone, enabled digest kinds, email verification and AI access. Look at **Today** to distinguish generation from an external delivery problem.

## Why are there fewer news stories than the limit?

The limit is a maximum. Pulse selects from available candidates, and a completed post can have no stories. **Quiet night** is an empty news result, not a scheduled day off.

## Why is Learning unavailable?

Choose an interest marked **Includes learning**, then enable Learning in **Settings → Digest**. See [Tips and topics](/docs/your-digest/tips-and-topics).

## Can you use your own sources?

Yes. Add public RSS or Atom feeds, or connect your own Resend receiving inbox, under **Sources → My sources**. Pulse does not create an email inbox for you.

## Can you pause just one destination?

Yes. Turn off its switch in **Settings → Delivery**. Your generated digests remain available in the app.

## Can you export your data?

There is no data export feature. You can [delete your account](/docs/account/delete-your-account) from Profile settings.
