You can suggest a personal feed for the shared catalog or propose a new interest. Suggestions wait for human review before being added.

## Suggest a feed

First [add the feed as your own source](/docs/interests-and-sources/add-your-own-feed). Under **Sources → My sources**, select **Suggest for the catalog** on that feed.

Choose an available **Interest**, write a **Reason** of 10 to 500 characters, then select **Send suggestion**.

Catalog suggestions must be public, readable feeds with at least three posts in the last 30 days. Feeds already in the catalog or previously proposed cannot be proposed again. Newsletter inboxes cannot be suggested through this action.

## Suggest an interest

Choose **Suggest an interest** during onboarding or under **Sources → By interest**. Enter a **Name** of 2 to 60 characters, a **Category** and a **Reason** of 10 to 500 characters, then select **Send suggestion**.

An approved interest still needs enough default sources before it becomes available to select.

## Suggestion limits

You can have up to 10 suggestions waiting for review, counting feeds and interests together. Suggestion submissions allow six requests per minute.
