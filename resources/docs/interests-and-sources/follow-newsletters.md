You can connect a Resend receiving inbox as a personal news source. Pulse reads received newsletter subjects, excerpts and links when preparing news.

## Prepare your inbox

Set up email receiving in your own Resend account, then subscribe newsletters to that receiving address or arrange forwarding to it. Pulse does not create an inbox address for you.

## Connect it to Pulse

1. Open **Sources → My sources → Add a source**.
2. Choose **Newsletter inbox**.
3. Enter your **Resend API key**, a **Name** and at least one interest.
4. Select **Test now**, then **Add** to save.

The key must have access to received emails. Pulse uses the key saved on this source to read the receiving inbox. Its credentials are stored encrypted.

## Update a saved inbox

Choose **Edit** under **My sources**. Leave the key blank to keep the saved key, or enter a replacement. To use **Test now** in this dialog, enter the key again.

> [!NOTE]
> Newsletter excerpts and links can be sent to your AI provider for news selection. Connect an inbox intended for the newsletters you want Pulse to read.
