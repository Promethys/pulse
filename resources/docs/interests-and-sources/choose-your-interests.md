Pulse has 50+ interests, grouped by category. Your choices add the default catalog sources for those interests and help the AI choose relevant news.

## Choose available interests

During onboarding, use **Search interests** to find a subject and select it. Choose at least one to continue. Interests marked **Coming soon** cannot be selected yet.

An **Includes learning** label means the interest can make daily tips and topics available to your account.

## Change them later

Open **Sources → By interest → Add or remove interests**, update your selection and save it.

Adding an interest adds its available default sources. Removing an interest removes enabled catalog subscriptions that no longer match your remaining interests. Your custom sources stay, and sources you explicitly switched off are not automatically switched back on.

Review the source switches after changing interests. If a subject is missing, use [Suggest an interest](/docs/interests-and-sources/suggest-a-source-or-interest).
