Open **Settings → Digest** to change what Pulse prepares and when it starts.

![Digest settings, with the news and learning times](/images/shots/digest-desktop.webp)

## Choose your times

Set **Timezone**, then choose **News time** and **Learning time**. Times are available in 15-minute steps. Select **Save** to apply your changes.

Pulse checks every 15 minutes and begins preparing each due post. The selected time is not a guaranteed delivery time.

## Limit news stories

Use **Max items per day** to choose from 1 to 20 news stories. New accounts start at 20. You may receive fewer, and this setting does not limit the learning tip and topic.

## Pause a digest kind

Turn off **News** or **Learning**, then select **Save**. You can turn it back on later. Learning needs an interest marked **Includes learning**.

There is no weekday or quiet-day schedule. A **Quiet night** message means a completed news post had no selected stories, not that you scheduled a day off.

To pause only email or one Discord destination, use its switch in **Settings → Delivery** instead. The digest can still be prepared and read in the app.
