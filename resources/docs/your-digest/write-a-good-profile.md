Your **About you** text tells the AI what matters to you and how to explain it. It is optional and can be up to 2,000 characters.

## What to include

Describe your experience, the subjects or tools you care about, and what you want to avoid. A couple of clear sentences is enough.

For example:

> I build web applications and want practical explanations of databases and accessibility. Keep summaries short and skip cryptocurrency stories.

Your interests and subscriptions decide which sources supply candidates. About you helps the AI choose and explain content from those candidates; it does not add sources.

## Where to edit it

Set it during the **About you** onboarding step, or open **Settings → Digest → About you** and select **Save** after editing.

This text is separate from the name and email address in **Settings → Profile**. It affects future generation, not already saved posts.

> [!NOTE]
> This text is sent to your AI provider. Include reading preferences rather than secrets or sensitive details.
