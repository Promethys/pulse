Your news post collects selected stories from your enabled subscriptions, grouped into sections by tag.

## What you see

Each story has a title, a link to the original and, when available, a short summary. Source names and tags help you see where it came from and find related saved stories.

Pulse asks your AI provider to select and summarise candidates using your interests, About you text and news item limit. It does not include every story a source publishes.

## Fewer stories or no stories

**Max items per day** is a ceiling, not a target. You may receive fewer stories. A completed news post with no selected items appears as **Quiet night** in the app.

This is different from a post that is still being prepared or whose generation failed. Those states have their own messages.

## Headlines without summaries

If the AI chain is exhausted by temporary availability or credit errors, Pulse can save source headlines without AI summaries. Other errors can fail generation instead.

Use [Sources](/docs/interests-and-sources/manage-sources) to change what enters selection and [About you](/docs/your-digest/write-a-good-profile) to guide the AI.
