The learning post contains one short practical tip and one deeper topic. It is separate from your news post and has its own schedule.

## Enable learning

Choose at least one interest marked **Includes learning** on **Sources**. Then open **Settings → Digest**, turn on **Learning**, choose its time and select **Save**.

Without an eligible interest, the Learning switch is unavailable.

## How the content is chosen

Pulse rotates through its learning topics, favouring topics you have not used or have not seen for longer, and tries to vary the category from the previous day.

Your About you text helps the AI explain the material for your experience and stack. Recent tip titles are included to help avoid repetition. The topic can include an **In practice:** paragraph and further reading links.

## Read it later

Tips and topics appear in the day view and in **Learning**, where you can filter saved entries. See [Learning](/docs/reading/learning).

The news item limit does not limit the tip and topic. Learning requires working AI access and has no headlines-only fallback.
