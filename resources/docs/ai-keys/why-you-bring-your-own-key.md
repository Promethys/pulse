Pulse uses an AI provider to select news and write learning content. Your own API key connects your digests to your provider account.

## What the key does

You choose the provider and model. Pulse uses your saved keys for your own digest requests and key tests. Keys are stored encrypted; the saved key list shows only the last four characters.

Your provider may charge you for these requests. Some providers have free tiers; check their terms and your account's billing settings. See [What it costs](/docs/ai-keys/what-it-costs).

## What the provider receives

News requests include your About you text, interests and source titles, excerpts and links. Learning requests include your profile and recent tip titles to help tailor the content and avoid repetition.

## Can you skip adding a key?

You can finish onboarding without one. Scheduled digests need a personal key unless your account has instance AI access. The AI keys page shows when Pulse's own key is being used.

When you have personal keys, Pulse uses that chain without falling back to instance keys. See [Add and order your keys](/docs/ai-keys/add-and-order-your-keys).
