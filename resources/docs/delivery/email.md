Send news, learning or both to one email address. You can enable email during onboarding or add it later.

## Add email delivery

1. Open **Settings → Delivery → Email → Add email**.
2. Enter **Email address**.
3. Under **Send to this destination**, choose **News**, **Learning** or both.
4. Select **Add**.
5. Use **Test** on the saved destination to send a real test email.

Onboarding email delivery includes both digest kinds. Learning still needs to be available and enabled in your digest settings.

## Change or pause delivery

You can save one email destination. Edit **Email address** and leave the field to save the change. Changes to the selected digest kinds save automatically.

Turn off the destination's switch to pause email without stopping your digests in the app. **Delete** removes the email destination.

The delivery address is managed separately from your account email in **Settings → Profile**.

## If no email arrives

Check the address, spam folder, destination switch and selected digest kinds. Check **Today** to see whether the digest itself was generated.

Delivery tests allow six per minute and 20 per hour per account, shared across delivery destinations. If testing still fails, [report the problem](/docs/help/contact-and-bug-reports) without sharing private addresses or message content.
