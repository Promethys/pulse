Your saved digests are available in Pulse without setting up an external destination. In-app delivery is always on.

## Read your digest

Open **Today** for the day view. If today has no saved post yet, Pulse shows your latest saved day when one exists. Check the displayed date.

Use **Archive** for previous days, **Search** to find saved stories and learning, and **Learning** for tips and topics.

## Add other destinations

You can also send news, learning or both to email and Discord in **Settings → Delivery**. Turning off an external destination does not turn off in-app reading.

If external delivery fails, a generated post can still be available in the app. Check **Today** before troubleshooting your [email destination](/docs/delivery/email) or [Discord webhook](/docs/delivery/discord-webhooks).
