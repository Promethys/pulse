Pulse turns the sources you follow into a daily news post. If your interests include learning, you can also receive a short tip and a deeper topic each day.

## Make it yours

Choose your interests, review their sources and add an optional About you text. Your AI provider uses these choices to select and explain content for you.

You can add your own feeds and newsletter inboxes alongside the shared source catalog.

## Read where you like

Your saved digests are available in the app. You can also send news, learning or both to Discord and email.

You choose the timezone and separate times for news and learning. Start with [Create your account](/docs/getting-started/create-your-account).
