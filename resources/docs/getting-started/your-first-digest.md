Your first digest depends on your interests, enabled sources, schedule and AI access. Finishing setup does not immediately generate a preview.

## Complete setup

1. **Interests**: select at least one available interest, then choose **Continue**.
2. **Sources**: turn off any default sources you do not want.
3. **About you**: optionally describe what you want to read, and choose your timezone.
4. **AI key**: choose a provider and model, enter a key and give consent to save it. You can use **Test the key** first.
5. **Delivery**: keep reading in the app, optionally enable email, then choose **Go to Today**.

You can use **Skip for now** on the About you and AI key steps. An AI key is required for scheduled digests unless your account has instance AI access.

## Wait for your scheduled time

Pulse checks every 15 minutes and starts preparing a digest once its local time is due. If you finish setup after that time, it can prepare today's digest at a later check that day.

Open **Today** to read saved content and check your setup. If a post is being prepared, refresh shortly. If generation fails, check **Settings → AI keys** and your enabled sources.

Learning also needs an interest marked **Includes learning**. See [When digests arrive](/docs/getting-started/when-digests-arrive).
