Your first digest depends on your interests, enabled sources, schedule and AI access. Finishing setup does not generate a digest right away, but Pulse starts fetching your sources immediately.

## Complete setup

1. **Interests**: select at least one available interest, then choose **Continue**.
2. **Sources**: turn off any default sources you do not want.
3. **About you**: optionally describe what you want to read. The timezone is prefilled from your browser and kept if you use **Skip for now**.
4. **AI key**: choose a provider and model, enter a key and give consent to save it. You can use **Test the key** first.
5. **Delivery**: keep reading in the app, optionally enable email, then choose **Go to Today**.

You can use **Skip for now** on the About you and AI key steps. An AI key is required for scheduled digests unless your account has instance AI access.

## See headlines right after setup

Finishing setup starts a fetch of your sources. Open **Today**: under **From your sources so far**, Pulse lists up to 10 headlines from the last 24 hours, with the source name under each title. Links open the original articles.

This list uses no AI, so it appears even without a key. Until the first fetch finishes, it says collecting headlines is in progress and to refresh in a minute.

## Generate your first digest now

With an AI key or instance AI access, **Today** offers **Generate my first digest now**. It runs the AI once and sends the digest to your destinations now, instead of at the scheduled time.

The button appears only before your first news digest. It needs a verified email, completed setup, news enabled and digests not paused.

## Wait for your scheduled time

Pulse checks every 15 minutes and starts preparing a digest once its local time is due. If you finish setup after that time, it can prepare today's digest at a later check that day.

Open **Today** to read saved content and check your setup. If a post is being prepared, refresh shortly. If generation fails, check **Settings → AI keys** and your enabled sources.

Learning also needs an interest marked **Includes learning**. See [When digests arrive](/docs/getting-started/when-digests-arrive).
