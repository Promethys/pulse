Create an account with your name, email address and a password, then verify your email before setting up your digest.

## Sign up

1. Choose **Create an account** on the public site.
2. Fill in **Name**, **Email address**, **Password** and **Confirm password**. Follow the password requirements shown in the form.
3. Select **Create account**.
4. Open the verification email and follow its link.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and use **Resend the email** on the verification page.

> [!NOTE]
> Registration can be closed on a Pulse instance. When it is closed, the sign-up page and account creation links are unavailable. Existing accounts can still log in.

## Use Google or GitHub

If available, select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub** and approve sign-in with that provider. Pulse uses your verified provider email, so you can go straight to setting up your digest. If the email already belongs to a Pulse account, sign-in links to that account instead. See [Sign in with Google or GitHub](/docs/account/profile-and-appearance#sign-in-with-google-or-github) for details and how to set a password later.

## Set up your digest

After verification, onboarding takes you through **Interests**, **Sources**, **About you**, **AI key** and **Delivery**. See [Your first digest](/docs/getting-started/your-first-digest) for what each step does.
