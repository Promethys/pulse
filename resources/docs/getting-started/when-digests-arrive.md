News and learning have separate daily schedules, both using your account's selected timezone.

## Starting times

New accounts start with news at **09:00**, learning at **09:30**, and **UTC** as the timezone. Choose your timezone during onboarding or in **Settings → Digest**.

Pulse checks every 15 minutes. When a post is due, it starts preparing it; AI generation and delivery can take longer.

## Change your schedule

Open **Settings → Digest**, choose **Timezone**, adjust **News time** or **Learning time**, and select **Save**. Times are available in 15-minute steps.

Use the News and Learning switches to pause either kind. Learning requires an interest marked **Includes learning**.

## If nothing arrives

Check that your email is verified, onboarding is complete, the digest kind is enabled and you have AI access. Then check **Today**: external delivery can fail even when your post is saved in the app.

See [Add and order your keys](/docs/ai-keys/add-and-order-your-keys) and [Email](/docs/delivery/email) for the relevant settings.
