Tags connect related stories in your saved news. Open a news tag to browse stories carrying that tag.

![A tag page, with its items and activity chart](/images/shots/tag-desktop.webp)

## Read a tag page

The page lists your matching news stories newest first, with their digest dates. Use pagination to reach older results.

When there are matching stories, a small chart shows weekly activity over the last 12 weeks. It counts stories in your digests with this tag, rather than activity across the whole source catalog.

## Filter with tags

In [Search](/docs/reading/search), you can select multiple tags. A result can match any selected tag.

In [Learning](/docs/reading/learning), select a tag to narrow saved tips and topics. Select it again to remove the filter. News tag pages themselves list news stories.
