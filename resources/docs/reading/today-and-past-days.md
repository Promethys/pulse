**Today** at `/dashboard` opens your daily reading view using the timezone in your digest settings.

The Pulse logo returns to the public site at `/`, even when you are signed in. Choose **Open Pulse** there to return to Today.

![The Today page, with its previous, calendar and next buttons](/images/shots/today-desktop.webp)

## Check the date

If there is no saved post for today, Pulse shows your latest saved day when one exists. The date heading tells you which day you are reading.

Tips and topics appear above news grouped into sections. Story links open the original articles.

## Move between days

Use **Previous day** and **Next day** to move between saved dates. Choose **Pick a date** to open the calendar. Only dates with saved digests can be selected.

For a list of saved days, open [Archive](/docs/reading/archive).

## Understand an empty day

A post being prepared asks you to refresh shortly. A failed post points you to AI keys and enabled sources. **Quiet night** means a completed news post has no selected stories.

Before your first digest, Today shows setup guidance. Check your interests, sources and AI access, then review [When digests arrive](/docs/getting-started/when-digests-arrive).
