**Search** finds content saved in your own digests. It searches news titles and summaries, or learning titles and bodies.

## Choose what to search

Select **News** or **Learning**, then type into the search field. Results update as you type. You can also leave the text empty and browse with filters.

## Narrow the results

| Filter      | Available for                                      |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Tags        | News and learning; matches any selected tag.       |
| Source      | News.                                              |
| Kind        | Learning: tips or topics.                          |
| Category    | Learning topics only.                              |
| From and To | Both, by the date content appeared in your digest. |

Dates refer to your digest, not the original article's publication date.

On a small screen, open **Filters** and use **Show results** to return to the list; the button includes the current result count. On larger screens, filters sit beside the results.

## Clear a search

Use **Clear search** to remove the words. **Clear filters**, or **Reset** in the mobile filter panel, removes filters while keeping your search text.

If there are no matches, try fewer filters or a broader word. Pagination lets you browse additional results.
