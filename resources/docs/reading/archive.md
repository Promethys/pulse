**Archive** lists your saved digest days, newest first. News and learning from the same date share a day entry.

![The Archive page, with one row per saved day](/images/shots/archive-desktop.webp)

## Open a day

Choose a row to read that day's content. The list shows the news item count, tags and post status information. Use the pagination controls to browse older days.

The archive includes days with pending, failed or empty posts, so a listed day does not always mean a completed news selection.

## Find something specific

Use [Search](/docs/reading/search) for words, tags, sources and date ranges. Use [Learning](/docs/reading/learning) to browse tips and topics separately.

If the archive is empty, open **Today** to check your setup and next digest.
